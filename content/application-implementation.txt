# **Application: Implementation Plan**

# *Translating evidence into actionable implementation strategy*


## **Solution Design Summary**

### **Final Solution Description**

**Solution Name:** *CARE Reset Program* (Comprehensive Approach to Resilience & Engagement)

**Core Components:**

1. **Workload Optimization Toolkit** – scheduling adjustments, task redistribution, streamlined documentation.
2. **Resilience & Well-Being Training** – mindfulness, stress management, emotional regulation, peer support.
3. **Leadership Coaching for Burnout Prevention** – supervisor training on supportive leadership and workload oversight.
4. **Burnout Early-Warning Dashboard** – real-time monitoring of staff well-being indicators.

**Evidence Base:**
This solution integrates evidence from organizational psychology (showing supportive leadership and workload control reduce burnout), healthcare workforce studies (highlighting the impact of staffing patterns and workflow inefficiencies), and implementation science (indicating that multi-component interventions outperform single-strategy approaches). Focus group findings from staff further shaped the final design to ensure feasibility and acceptability.


### **Theory of Change**

**Problem:**
Healthcare employees experience high burnout due to chronic understaffing, emotional strain, inefficient workflows, and limited access to well-being support.

**Inputs:**
Training materials, facilitators, leadership support, HR and scheduling data, funding for technology, staff time, and IT integration resources.

**Activities:**
Provide resilience training, redesign workflows, coach leaders, monitor burnout indicators, and conduct regular check-ins.

**Outputs:**
Number of staff trained, new scheduling processes implemented, leadership coaching sessions completed, and operational dashboards launched.

**Outcomes:**
Improved staff well-being, reduced emotional exhaustion, better team climate, improved job satisfaction, and lower absenteeism.

**Impact:**
A healthier, more stable, and more productive healthcare workforce with reduced turnover and improved patient care quality.

**Assumptions:**
Staff will participate, leadership will enforce workload changes, and technology support will remain consistent.

**External Factors:**
Staff shortages, fluctuating patient volumes, union requirements, regulatory changes, and external crises (e.g., pandemics).

## **Implementation Planning**

### **Phase 1: Preparation**

**Timeline: Month 1–2**

### **Preparation Activities**

**Activity 1: Assemble Implementation Team**

* **Purpose:** Ensure cross-functional oversight and accountability.
* **Deliverables:** Implementation team roster, roles, and responsibilities.
* **Resources Needed:** Leadership time, HR representative, IT support.
* **Success Criteria:** Team formally approved and operational.

**Activity 2: Conduct Baseline Assessments**

* **Purpose:** Establish burnout, workload, and satisfaction baselines.
* **Deliverables:** Baseline report summarizing survey and HR data.
* **Resources Needed:** Survey software, HR data analysts.
* **Success Criteria:** At least 70% staff response rate.

**Activity 3: Customize Training Content**

* **Purpose:** Tailor resilience and leadership training to organizational needs.
* **Deliverables:** Final training modules, handouts, facilitator scripts.
* **Resources Needed:** Training designers, facilitators.
* **Success Criteria:** Leadership sign-off on curriculum.

### **Preparation Phase Milestones**

* Implementation team formed
* Baseline data collected
* Training finalized and approved

### **Stakeholder Engagement During Preparation**

**Management/Leadership:**

* **Communication:** Overview of program goals, expected commitments, and timelines.
* **Decision Points:** Approve curriculum, resource allocation, and scheduling adjustments.
* **Support Required:** Visible endorsement and prioritization.

**Employees/Staff:**

* **Communication:** Purpose of program, participation expectations, and timelines.
* **Involvement:** Provide baseline feedback and participate in pilot sessions.
* **Training/Support:** Orientation on program benefits.

**Customers/Clients:**

* **Communication:** Only as needed if patient workflow changes occur.
* **Feedback Opportunities:** Patient surveys (optional if workflows shift).

**Partners/Suppliers:**

* **Communication:** Inform EHR vendors or workflow partners of upcoming changes.
* **Coordination:** Align training schedules and technology integration.

### **Phase 2: Initial Implementation**

**Timeline: Month 3–4**

### **Initial Implementation Activities**

**Activity 1: Pilot Resilience Training and Leadership Coaching**

* **Purpose:** Test and refine training before full rollout.
* **Deliverables:** Pilot report with feedback and improvement recommendations.
* **Resources Needed:** Facilitators, training rooms, digital materials.
* **Success Criteria:** 80% satisfaction from pilot participants.
* **Risk Mitigation:** Add make-up sessions, adjust content based on feedback.

**Activity 2: Implement Workload Optimization Toolkit on 1–2 Units**

* **Purpose:** Identify operational barriers early.
* **Deliverables:** Revised scheduling templates, workflow maps.
* **Resources Needed:** Unit managers, scheduling analysts.
* **Success Criteria:** Reduction in unnecessary tasks or documentation by 10%.
* **Risk Mitigation:** Frequent check-ins to address resistance.

### **Initial Implementation Milestones**

* Pilot training complete
* Leadership coaching launched
* Workload redesign tested

### **Early Success Indicators**

**Process Indicators:**
High staff attendance, timely completion of coaching, improved scheduling cohesion.

**Outcome Indicators:**
Small reductions in stress reports, improved perceptions of support.

**Stakeholder Response Indicators:**
Positive feedback during debrief sessions, increased engagement in pilot.

### **Phase 3: Full Implementation**

**Timeline: Month 5–8**

### **Full Implementation Activities**

**Activity: Organization-wide Rollout**

* **Purpose:** Scale successful components across all units.
* **Deliverables:** Full training completion, updated workflows, functioning burnout dashboard.
* **Resources Needed:** Trainers, IT specialists, unit managers.
* **Success Criteria:** 85% training completion rate; full dashboard activation.
* **Risk Mitigation:** Staggered rollout to avoid operational disruptions.

### **Full Implementation Milestones**

* All units trained
* All leaders coached
* Dashboard implemented organization-wide

### **Success Monitoring**

**KPI 1:** Burnout reduction – *Target: 15% decrease*
**KPI 2:** Job satisfaction increase – *Target: 10% increase*
**KPI 3:** Absenteeism reduction – *Target: 8% decrease*

### **Phase 4: Evaluation & Adjustment**

**Timeline: Month 9–10**

### **Evaluation Activities**

**Process Evaluation:**
Assess fidelity to training delivery, adherence to workload redesign, and leadership engagement.

**Outcome Evaluation:**
Compare pre- and post-intervention burnout, satisfaction, and absenteeism.

**Impact Evaluation:**
Assess improvements in team climate, turnover trends, and patient care indicators.

**Stakeholder Satisfaction Assessment:**
Collect feedback on perceived usefulness and sustainability.

### **Adjustment Planning**

**Successful Elements to Maintain:**
Leadership coaching, burnout dashboard, streamlined workflows.

**Elements Needing Modification:**
Training frequency, communication patterns, time allotted for resilience activities.

**Elements to Discontinue:**
Any training modules or processes viewed as redundant or ineffective.

## **Technology and Infrastructure Requirements**

**Technology Needs:**
Training platform, burnout dashboard software, scheduling optimization tools.

**Physical Infrastructure Needs:**
Training rooms, quiet spaces for resilience exercises.

**Information Systems Requirements:**
Integration with HR systems, EHR data where applicable, automated reporting tools.

## **Communication Plan**

### **Communication Objectives**

* **Awareness:** Ensure all staff understand program purpose, timing, and expectations.
* **Buy-in:** Highlight leadership commitment and evidence supporting burnout reduction.
* **Coordination:** Align unit schedules with training and workflow redesign.
* **Feedback:** Provide channels for ongoing staff concerns and suggestions.

## **Sustainability Planning**

### **Ensuring Long-term Success**

**Institutionalization Strategy:**
Embed resilience training into onboarding, make burnout dashboard part of monthly leadership reviews.

**Continuous Improvement Process:**
Quarterly evaluations, annual updates to training content, ongoing staff feedback loops.

**Knowledge Transfer:**
Document all processes, create digital resource libraries, train internal champions.

### **Resource Sustainability**

**Ongoing Resource Requirements:**
Staff time for training refreshers, dashboard maintenance, periodic leadership coaching.

**Funding Strategy:**
Blend operational budgets, grants, and cost savings from reduced turnover.

**Efficiency Improvements:**
Automate reporting, streamline training modules, reduce administrative burden.
