# Assessment: Monitoring & Evaluation Plan
# *Systematic approach to measuring implementation success and organizational impact*

## **Evaluation Framework Overview**

### **Evaluation Questions**

**Primary Evaluation Question:**
Did the burnout-reduction intervention significantly decrease employee burnout levels among healthcare staff?

**Secondary Evaluation Questions:**

1. To what extent did the intervention improve overall staff well-being and job satisfaction?
2. How consistently and effectively was the intervention implemented across units or departments?
3. Were healthcare employees satisfied with the intervention’s content, delivery, and relevance?
4. Did any unintended consequences occur, such as increased workload, workflow disruption, or resistance to change?


### **Evaluation Approach**

**Evaluation Type:**
Both **formative** (to monitor progress during implementation) and **summative** (to assess final impact).

**Evaluation Design:**
A **before-and-after comparison** using pre-intervention and post-intervention burnout measures, supported by limited **with-and-without comparison** if comparable units exist.

**Mixed Methods Approach:**
Quantitative data (burnout scores, absenteeism, turnover, workload hours) will be combined with qualitative data (focus groups, interviews, open-ended survey questions) to triangulate findings and provide deeper insight into staff experiences.


## **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

### **Outcome KPIs (What Changed)**

#### **Primary Outcome KPI**

**KPI:** Reduction in burnout levels measured by the Maslach Burnout Inventory (MBI).

* **Current Baseline:** Average MBI emotional exhaustion score of staff before intervention.
* **Target:** 15–20% reduction in emotional exhaustion scores.
* **Timeline:** Expected within 6 months of full intervention implementation.
* **Data Source:** MBI survey results.
* **Collection Method:** Online or paper survey administered anonymously.
* **Measurement Frequency:** Baseline, mid-point (3 months), and post-intervention (6 months).

#### **Secondary Outcome KPIs**

**KPI 1:** Improvement in job satisfaction levels.

* **Current Baseline:** Scores from pre-intervention job satisfaction survey.
* **Target:** 10% increase in job satisfaction ratings.
* **Timeline:** Within 6 months.
* **Data Source & Method:** HR-administered job satisfaction survey.

**KPI 2:** Reduction in absenteeism.

* **Current Baseline:** Average sick-leave days per employee per quarter.
* **Target:** 8–10% reduction.
* **Timeline:** Within 9 months.
* **Data Source & Method:** HR attendance records extracted quarterly.

### **Process KPIs (How Implementation Went)**

#### **Implementation Quality KPIs**

**KPI:** Percentage of staff completing burnout-reduction training or workshops.

* **Target:** At least 85% participation.
* **Data Source:** Attendance logs.
* **Collection Method:** Sign-in sheets or digital attendance records.
* **Measurement Frequency:** Each training session.

**KPI:** Fidelity to intervention model (e.g., sessions delivered as designed).

* **Target:** 90% adherence to curriculum.
* **Data Source:** Facilitator checklists and supervisor observations.
* **Collection Method:** Structured fidelity monitoring forms.

#### **Stakeholder Engagement KPIs**

**KPI:** Staff satisfaction with the intervention.

* **Target:** 80% reporting “satisfied” or “very satisfied.”
* **Data Source:** Post-training satisfaction surveys.
* **Collection Method:** Anonymous electronic survey.

#### **Resource Utilization KPIs**

**KPI:** Efficiency of time and staffing resource use.

* **Target:** Intervention completed within 10% of planned resource budget.
* **Data Source:** Project management tracking tools and HR cost data.
* **Collection Method:** Monthly resource audits.


### **Impact KPIs (Broader Organizational Effects)**

#### **Organizational Performance KPIs**

**KPI:** Staff turnover rate reduction.

* **Current Baseline:** Annual turnover rate prior to intervention.
* **Target:** 5% reduction in turnover within one year.
* **Timeline:** 12 months post-intervention.
* **Data Source & Method:** HR turnover reports analyzed quarterly.

#### **Cultural/Climate KPIs**

**KPI:** Improvement in organizational climate (e.g., teamwork, psychological safety, communication).

* **Baseline:** Pre-intervention climate survey.
* **Target:** 10% improvement in climate scores.
* **Timeline:** Within 12 months.
* **Data Source & Method:** Annual organizational climate survey.

## **Data Collection Plan**

### **Quantitative Data Collection**

**Data Type 1:** Absenteeism rates.

* **Source:** HR attendance records.
* **Collection Frequency:** Quarterly.
* **Collection Method:** Automated HRIS data extraction.
* **Analysis Plan:** Compare pre- and post-intervention averages using paired statistics.

**Data Type 2:** Turnover rates.

* **Source:** HR separation reports.
* **Collection Frequency:** Quarterly.
* **Collection Method:** HRIS-generated turnover reports.
* **Analysis Plan:** Trend analysis over 12 months.

#### **Survey Data**

**Survey 1: Stakeholder Satisfaction Survey**

* **Target Population:** All healthcare staff participating in the intervention.
* **Sample Size Goal:** Minimum 60% response rate.
* **Timing:** Immediately after training sessions and again after full implementation.
* **Key Questions:** Satisfaction with training, perceived usefulness, relevance to burnout, confidence in applying skills.
* **Administration Method:** Online survey (email link).

### **Quantitative Analysis**

#### **Outcome Analysis**

**Primary Outcome Analysis:**

* **Statistical Approach:** Paired t-tests or repeated-measures ANOVA.
* **Comparison Strategy:** Pre-intervention vs. post-intervention burnout scores.
* **Significance Criteria:** Statistically significant improvement (p < .05) and practical improvement of at least 10–15%.

**Secondary Outcome Analysis:**

* **Approach:** Descriptive statistics and paired comparisons for absenteeism, satisfaction, and turnover.
* **Criteria:** Positive directional change consistent with expected outcomes.

## **Success Criteria and Interpretation**

### **Success Thresholds**

#### **Must-Achieve Criteria**

**Criterion 1:** Meaningful reduction in burnout levels.

* **Measurement:** Change in MBI emotional exhaustion score.
* **Threshold:** At least 15% improvement from baseline.

**Criterion 2:** High-quality implementation fidelity.

* **Measurement:** Fidelity checklist completion.
* **Threshold:** At least 90% adherence to intervention design.
